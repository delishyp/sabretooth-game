# Appearance-neutral lines v1

When the both-match gate fails (today that's every case, since all three leaders are flagged), these neutral lines play.

- Highcrag leader beat 2: "{catName} sits at the top of the path in the slanting light, utterly still, and doesn't rise. The leader of Highcrag doesn't need to."
- Bonefield leader beat 2: "{catName} walks out of the stems with heavy shoulders and a heavier stare, two guards flanking at a respectful distance. Neither guard looks anywhere but at you."
- Mistwood leader beat 3 narration: "{catName} steps out of the grey. {catName}'s eyes look like they've watched too many cats go under the water.", followed by the same speech.
- Repeat meeting injury clause (only when the save has a recorded injury, and no portrait check is needed since it's a scent): "{catName} carries the sharp, coppery smell of a wound still healing, and the smell of other cats is on the fur." This replaces the visible scratch line.
- Bonefield first meeting beat 2: replace "The cat is broad through the chest and scarred along the forelegs, every mark a challenge won or survived." with "{catName} stands like a cat who has won every fight that mattered, and expects to win this one." Then replace "letting you take in every scar" with "letting you take a good long look".

Sabers, heavy forelimbs, the bobtail and temporary states such as mud, wet fur or a scent need no gate.
