Bonefield Pride

“The weak feed the strong. That is the law of the grass.” Harsh, martial and loud. Bonefield cubs are trained to fight from the moment a cub can walk. Rank is won by challenge and held by scars. The pride hunts the biggest prey in the Vale and takes the most losses doing it. Strangers are met with a show of force, and a stranger who stands ground earns respect.

The leader of Bonefield

Cragfoot leads Bonefield Pride, a heavy tom with a torn lip who won his place by challenge and has held it against every challenger since.
