# For Anna: four systems to sign off (Tundra, 2026-11-10)
These are plain summaries of the four specs Director asked me to bring to you. The full specs are linked. Nothing gets built until you sign each one off. Director will ask the questions one at a time.

## 1. Reputation and renown (`reputation-v0.1.md` v0.1.2, written by Saga, tuned by me)
- Cats remember what you do on three axes: **Courage** (brave or craven), **Mercy** (merciful or cruel) and **Honour** (true or oathbreaker).
- Only deeds someone saw or heard about count. Gossip spreads it.
- It builds slowly. First come whispers ("Whispered to be…"), then a title like *Name the Brave*. An active player who sticks to one way of playing earns a title after about four in-game years, and the top level takes most of a lifetime.
- A title can be lost through opposite deeds, never just from time passing. Some premade cats start with a title from their history.
- **Questions:** should reputation fade over a lifetime? (Right now it doesn't.) Should secrets coming out, such as a cat's hidden past, become reputation moments later?

## 2. World map and territory (`world-map-territory-v0.1.md` v0.1.3, on `map-v1.md`)
- There are 30 regions on one painted Vale. Travel takes one or two days per step, and some passes are slower in winter.
- Every region shows its danger in words (Safe, Uneasy, Dangerous, Deadly) before you go.
- The prides claim, patrol, mark and fight over land, so the map really changes, about one region a season. You hear about changes through gossip and scent, not pop-ups.
- Human camps sit at the salt flats and camel ridge, and hyena dens at the tar seeps and south range.
- **Question:** each pride ends up holding 3 to 6 regions, not the 2 to 4 we first planned. With 13-cat prides, fewer regions left them hungry in the sim. Is that OK?

## 3. Lore Book (`lore-book-v0.1.md`)
- This is your cat's journal of everything known: cats, places, prides, promises and debts, and rumours. Each entry says how you learned it.
- Rumours can be **false**. A cat who dislikes someone sometimes passes on a lie about them, and you can't tell until you find out for yourself.
- **Question:** should a cat with a grudge lie about 1 time in 4, or should lies be rarer?

## 4. Councils (`councils-v0.1.md`, new: two older drafts merged)
- Once a season the three pride leaders meet at Split Rock to settle land fights and feuds. If a feud boils over, they meet early.
- They can agree to a truce, a border deal, a shared hunting ground or an alliance, plus a few smaller favours.
- Keeping a deal builds trust. Breaking one makes every pride hear about it and hurts your Honour.
- Once you found a pride you get a seat, and a vote once at least two prides accept you. Before that you can only be summoned.
- **Questions:** is once a season plus emergencies the right pace? As a loner, should you be able to ask to come?
